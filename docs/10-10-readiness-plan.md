## Boundary for a true public 10/10

A fully public 10/10 government-infrastructure website should add only approved source material:

- Factory exterior, entrance, production floor, testing area and charger photographs.
- Product datasheets with model names, ratings, connector types, protocol support, warranty and compliance evidence.
- Manufacturing capacity, machinery, quality-process and certification records.
- Corporate registrations, legal identifiers and final legal review for policy pages.
- Approved government, PSU, institutional or private project records with client permission.
- Security review for persistent enquiry storage, malware scanning and secure document exchange.
- Lighthouse, browser compatibility and accessibility checks on the production domain.

## Publication rule

Do not move any item from controlled evidence to public evidence until the source record is approved for public use.
