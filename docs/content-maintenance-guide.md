# Content Maintenance Guide

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

## Editing principles

- Keep government and institutional buyers as the primary audience.
- Use clear Indian English.
- Avoid consumer-sales, discount, franchise-first and guaranteed-return language.
- Do not publish claims that are not backed by approved source documents.
- Keep private-sector pages as a secondary pathway.

## Monthly review

- Check all public CTAs.
- Check downloads and document versions.
- Review sitemap and robots output.
- Review redirect map for newly discovered historical URLs.
- Review Knowledge Centre dates and policy relevance.
- Review form submissions and spam patterns after CRM integration.

## Before adding a project case study

Confirm:

- Project name.
- Location.
- Client or partner publication permission.
- Project type.
- Charger configuration.
- EHUB Bharat scope.
- Commercial model.
- Current status.
- Start and completion dates.
- Approved outcomes.
- Approved photographs.
- Reference contact availability, where approved.

Use status labels: Concept, Proposed, Feasibility, Pilot, Under Implementation, Commissioned or Operational.

## Before adding product details

Confirm:

- Approved datasheet.
- Product rating.
- Connector and protocol information.
- Safety and compliance evidence.
- Warranty and service terms.
- Source document owner and review date.

Never publish "largest", "number one", "government approved", "empanelled", "certified", "indigenous", "fully localized" or "industry-leading" unless documentary evidence supports the statement.
