# EHUB Bharat Website Rebuild - Executive Summary

Last reviewed: 2026-07-26

## What changed

The website has been rebuilt around a government-first institutional narrative:

- EHUB Bharat is positioned as an integrated EV charging infrastructure and EV charger manufacturing partner for governments, public-sector organizations and institutions.
- The primary delivery story is Plan - Manufacture - Deploy - Operate.
- The Telangana EV charger manufacturing facility is prominently presented using the supplied address.
- Private-sector pathways remain available, but they are secondary to government, institutional and public-infrastructure project flows.
- The Government Project Desk form replaces generic partner language on the primary conversion path.
- The release now includes executive assurance, procurement confidence, tender evaluation and controlled-evidence sections for government reviewers.

## Claim-control decision

The live website does not publish unsupported claims about capacity, certifications, product ratings, government approvals, contracts, project locations, uptime, employee count, awards, partnerships, factory size, export activity or revenue.

## Launch condition

Before public domain migration, EHUB Bharat should supply approved product datasheets, facility photographs, legal identifiers, corporate registrations, certifications, real project records and final legal review for policy pages.
