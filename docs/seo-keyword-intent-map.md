## SEO controls

- No keyword stuffing.
- No mass-produced doorway pages.
- No unverified project, capacity, certification, rating or approval claims.
- Page metadata is unique and aligned with the route's intent.
- Structured data uses Corporation, Service, Place, BreadcrumbList and ItemList/Article where appropriate.
